📖 How to Play Pallanguzhi (விளக்கம்)

1. The Board & Setup (அமைப்பு): There are 14 carved cups (7 per player) containing 5 tamarind seeds (புளியங்கொட்டை) each. Player 1 controls the bottom row; Player 2 / AI controls the top row.
2. Sowing (விதைத்தல்): Tap any cup on your side with seeds. All seeds are scooped up and distributed one-by-one counter-clockwise into subsequent cups.
3. Continuous Scooping (தொடர் சுழற்சி): When your hand empties, look at the very next cup. If it contains seeds, you pick them all up and continue sowing without pausing!
4. The Empty Cup Capture (துடைத்தல்): If your hand runs out of seeds and the next cup is EMPTY, your turn ends. But you immediately capture all the seeds in the cup following that empty one into your store!
5. The "Pasu" Rule (பசு - 4 Seeds Bonus): Whenever any pit on the board reaches exactly 4 seeds during sowing, the owner of that side immediately collects those 4 seeds into their bank.
