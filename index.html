📖 How to play · விதிகள்

1. Board: Each player owns 7 cups. Every cup starts with 5 seeds (70 total). The outer cups store captured seeds and are not part of the sowing path.

2. Your move · விதைத்தல்: Select a nonempty cup from your own row. Pick up all its seeds and sow one into each next cup, counter-clockwise. You may sow into both rows.

3. Keep sowing · தொடர் சுழற்சி: When your hand becomes empty, examine the next cup. If it has seeds, pick them up and continue from the following cup.

4. Capture · துடைத்தல்: If that next cup is empty, your turn ends. Capture all seeds in the cup immediately after the empty one, if any. They go to the current player's store.

5. Pasu · பசு: Whenever sowing makes a cup contain exactly 4 seeds, its owner immediately collects those 4 seeds into their store, even during an opponent's move.

6. Match end: When a player's seven cups are all empty at the end of a turn, this single-round variant ends. Each player collects the seeds left in their own row. Most seeds wins.

7. Smart play (v3.5): Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert when facing the computer. Tap 💡 Hint on your turn for a suggested cup; the suggestion does not play for you. Tap ↶ Undo to restore the previous human decision (including the computer's reply in AI mode). Undo also works during an animation.

In v3.6.1, Player 1’s hand enters from below and points toward the top plank, while Player 2’s hand mirrors it from above. The hand orientation follows the rotated board on phones. Regional Pallanguzhi rules can vary; this game deliberately follows the six rules above. Completed moves conserve exactly 70 seeds. Nonterminating moves are rejected instead of applying a partial turn. The 🔊 Sound button plays short procedurally rendered wood taps and bead-on-wood clacks (no internet required). Volume is adjustable in Match settings. An animated hand collects and distributes beads; collisions and bounces are visual approximations, not a full physics simulation. Pace cycles between Natural (default), Relaxed and Fast. The “Finish move” control completes the current move instantly without changing its result.
