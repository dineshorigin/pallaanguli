📖 How to Play Pallanguzhi (விளக்கம்)

1. The Objective: Collect more tamarind seeds (புளியங்கொட்டை) into your store than your opponent. Player 1 controls the bottom 7 pits; Player 2 controls the top 7 pits.
2. Sowing (விதைத்தல்): On your turn, tap any pit on your side with seeds. You pick them up and drop one seed into each pit counter-clockwise around the board.
3. Continuous Sowing: When your hand empties, look at the next pit. If it has seeds, you scoop them all up and continue sowing!
4. The Empty Pit Capture (துடைத்தல்): If your hand empties and the next pit is EMPTY, your turn ends, but you CAPTURE all the seeds in the cup immediately following it into your score!
5. Pasu (பசு - 4 Seeds Bonus): Whenever any pit accumulates exactly 4 seeds during sowing, the player who owns that side immediately captures those 4 seeds!
