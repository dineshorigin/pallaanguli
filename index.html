📖 How to Play Pallanguzhi (விளக்கம்)

1. Board & Territory: Player 1 owns the bottom 7 cups; Player 2 owns the top 7 cups. The two outer cups are the Thaaikuli (தாய்க்குழி) stores where captured seeds settle.
2. Sowing (விதைத்தல்): On your turn, tap any cup on your side with seeds. Scoop them up and sow one seed counter-clockwise into each subsequent cup.
3. Continuous Sowing (தொடர் சுழற்சி): When your hand empties, check the very next cup. If it contains seeds, scoop them all up and continue sowing!
4. Empty Pit Capture (துடைத்தல்): If your hand empties and the next cup is EMPTY, your turn ends, and you CAPTURE all seeds from the cup immediately following it into your store!
5. Pasu (பசு - 4 Seeds Bonus): Whenever any cup accumulates exactly 4 seeds during sowing, the owner of that side immediately captures those 4 seeds!
