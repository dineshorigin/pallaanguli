📖 How to Play Pallanguzhi (விளக்கம்)

1. Board & Territory: The bottom plank of 7 cups belongs to Player 1; the top plank of 7 cups belongs to Player 2. The two standing outer side cups are the Thaaikuli (தாய்க்குழி) stores to bank captured seeds.
2. Sowing (விதைத்தல்): On your turn, tap any cup on your glowing wooden plank that has seeds. You scoop them up and sow one seed counter-clockwise into each subsequent cup.
3. Continuous Sowing (தொடர் சுழற்சி): When your hand empties, check the very next cup. If it contains seeds, scoop them all up and continue sowing!
4. Empty Pit Capture (துடைத்தல்): If your hand empties and the next cup is EMPTY, your turn ends, and you CAPTURE all seeds from the cup immediately following it into your outer Thaaikuli!
5. Pasu (பசு - 4 Seeds Bonus): Whenever any cup accumulates exactly 4 seeds during sowing, the owner of that plank immediately captures those 4 seeds!
